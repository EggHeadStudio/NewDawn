# Asset catalog

All runtime geometry is original and procedural.

## Nature
- Streamed procedural terrain with original starter region, broad continental forms, and desert and polar/snow colors
- Conifer and broadleaf tree silhouettes with seasonal foliage
- Instanced grass and flowers
- Dodecahedron rocks
- Berry bushes
- Ocean, beach, grass, forest, rock, desert and snow biome colors; no authored rivers or water-travel assets yet

## Characters
- Articulated procedural player and seeded NPCs with shaped faces, layered clothing, cloaks, fur, braided hair, facial hair, hoods and helmets; ember, sage and azure palettes
- Six hostile variants share the humanoid rig, with distinct masks, weapons, robes, shields, armor, claws or horns; necromancer projectiles use a separate glowing mesh
- A rotatable appearance preview; the current meshes remain simplified and have no character LOD or shared geometry

## Wildlife
- Procedural deer, rabbit, wolf and bird models with articulated movement
- Eight requested species are not yet implemented

## Buildings
- Shelter
- Storage

- Generator
- Wind turbine
- Battery and cable post

## Transport
- Four-wheel rover

## Application
