# EggHeadStudio: New Dawn — Long-term vision

New Dawn is a low-poly open-world survival and civilization-building game. One survivor explores a procedural Earth-like planet, gathers food and materials, constructs a settlement, recruits inhabitants, builds engineering networks, develops transport, and eventually establishes a large civilization.

The long-term implementation includes planet-scale chunk streaming and wrapping longitude, hemispheric seasons, weather, editable terrain, local and remote simulation, NPC roles and needs, hostile raids, vehicles and trailers, boats and roads, mechanical/electrical/water networks, production chains, cooperative multiplayer, and PWA/offline play.
